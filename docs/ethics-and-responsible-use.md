# Ethics & Responsible Use

I am learning cybersecurity to **defend** systems. These are the rules I follow, and the rules for anyone using content from this repo:

1. **Permission first, always.** I only test systems I own (my home lab VMs) or systems that explicitly invite testing (CTFs, TryHackMe, Hack The Box, bug-bounty programs *within their stated scope*, intentionally-vulnerable apps like DVWA/Juice Shop running locally).
2. **No harm.** No DoS, no data theft/exfiltration, no persistence, no touching real user data — even where a lab would technically allow it, I stop at proof and document.
3. **Responsible disclosure.** If I ever find a real vulnerability in a real product, I report it privately to the vendor/program first, give reasonable time to fix, and only discuss it publicly in general/defensive terms after a fix.
4. **Defense in every write-up.** Every offensive technique I study in a lab gets a paired section: *How to detect it · How to prevent/fix it · What logs would show it.*
5. **No malware.** I do not write, store, or distribute malware, credential stealers, phishing kits, or bypass tools for unauthorized use.
6. **Respect the law & platform rules**, including the computer-misuse laws of Singapore and GitHub's Acceptable Use Policies.

If any content here ever looks inconsistent with the above, please open an issue and I will fix or remove it promptly.
